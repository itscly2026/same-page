# Retain personal layers temporarily after membership removal

Removing or leaving a choir immediately revokes cloud access and synchronization, but the former member's server-side personal layers remain recoverable for thirty days before deletion. Reinstating the same membership during that window restores them; already downloaded content cannot be remotely recalled, and unsynchronized local personal drafts are not silently deleted. This balances prompt authorization revocation with recovery from accidental removal without granting drive owners access to private annotations.

Public preview is an explicit exception to ordinary member reading: preview access does not depend on membership, but all experience annotations stay local even for signed-in users. Experience reads neither provision nor expose server personal layers; removing a preview membership immediately revokes its shared-layer grants, operation permissions and delegated scopes. Existing preview personal layers do not enter the thirty-day deletion period on membership removal, but that retention does not grant the experience entry cloud write access. ADR-0010 supersedes the former promise of cloud personal annotations through the public experience. Ordinary member reader entries retain their account workspace.

Only the owner or a member with remove/restore-members permission may reinstate a removed membership within the recovery window. Restoration retains the same membership identity but returns it as an ordinary member without old shared-layer grants. At expiry, ordinary-drive personal layers, user preferences and the removed membership are purged; a later join is a new membership. A pending user deletion holds identity and personal data until its own recovery deadline. The unique owner must transfer ownership before leaving, removal or user deletion; the database protects this invariant atomically.
